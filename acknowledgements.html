ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS & SI DISCLOSURE

Technology, Intelligence and Human Creativity Behind the Project

This project was developed through a combination of independent effort, scientific research and Super Intelligence (SI) tools. These technologies supported the research, organisation, programming and visual development of the website, helping transform the scientific story of the Kepler Space Telescope into an engaging educational experience.

01 · Tool Attribution

SUPER INTELLIGENCE TOOLS UTILISED

Five distinct Super Intelligence tools supported specific phases of research, editorial structuring, software development, visual direction, and disclosure refinement.

SI-01
TOOL 1 / Research and Information Gathering

ChatGPT (OpenAI)

ChatGPT was utilised to gather, explore and understand scientific information related to the Kepler Space Telescope, including its mission history, engineering principles, observational techniques, discoveries and scientific significance. It assisted in explaining complex astronomical concepts and identifying relevant information for the educational content.

SI-02
TOOL 2 / Content Organisation and Structuring

Gemini (Google)

Gemini assisted in organising the gathered information into a logical and structured format. It helped arrange scientific topics into chapters and subtopics, establish a coherent flow of information and improve the overall presentation of educational content.

SI-03
TOOL 3 / Coding and Programming Assistance

Claude (Anthropic)

Claude was utilised to assist with website coding, including generating, refining and debugging code, developing website components and supporting the implementation of interactive features and layouts.

SI-04
TOOL 4 / Website Vision and Development

Google Antigravity

Google Antigravity was used to develop and shape the overall vision of the website, supporting its visual direction, design approach, website structure and implementation workflow. It helped translate the project's concept into a cohesive digital experience.

SI-05
TOOL 5 / AI-Assisted Design and Development Support

Google AI Studio

Google AI Studio was utilised to support the development and refinement of website components, particularly the creation and improvement of the acknowledgement and SI disclosure page, while assisting with its presentation and content organisation.

05 · Authorship & Stewardship

THE HUMAN CONTRIBUTION

While Super Intelligence tools assisted with different aspects of development, the project was guided by human planning, creativity and decision-making. The project developer was responsible for establishing the concept, selecting the subject, defining the educational structure, directing the visual presentation, integrating the content and reviewing the resulting website. AI-assisted material was evaluated and refined as part of the development process.

06 · Scientific Integrity

RESPONSIBLE USE AND TRANSPARENCY

Super Intelligence tools were used as supportive technologies to accelerate research, content development and website creation. Their outputs are not presented as official NASA publications, endorsements or original scientific observations. Scientific explanations are intended to be checked against credible sources, including NASA's official mission documentation and astronomical data resources.

Compliance Statement

AI-generated images and videos are visibly identified. AI-assisted code, audio and data are disclosed in appropriate documentation. No NASA branding, logos, flags or official mission identifiers are generated or modified through AI tools.

Behind every discovery lies curiosity, creativity and the pursuit of knowledge. By combining scientific exploration with modern technology, this project aims to make the remarkable journey of the Kepler Space Telescope accessible to everyone.
