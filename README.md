
 # 🌌 EXO WORLDS — Inside Kepler

> *Step beyond our sky and witness the untold story of a lone machine that changed how we see the universe.*

**EXO WORLDS** is an interactive 3D web experience created by **Team Dormant** for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge**. It transforms the historic discoveries and engineering behind NASA’s Kepler Space Telescope into an immersive, scrollytelling journey.

🔗 **Live App:** [exoworldkepler.netlify.app](https://exoworldkepler.netlify.app/)  
💻 **Repository:** [GitHub — Team Dormant](https://github.com/AgnesJerusha/exo-worlds---inside-kepler--1-)

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Tribute to the Pioneers:** Honors Dr. William Borucki, David Koch, and the NASA Kepler Mission Team.
- **Chapter 1 — Awakening the Cosmos:** Interactive transit photometry and real-time flux visualizations.
- **Chapter 2 — Inside Kepler:** 3D exploration of the 95-MP photometer, reaction wheel challenges, and the K2 solar-pressure revival.
- **Chapter 3 — Discoveries & Legacy:** Interactive 3D concepts of **Kepler-22b** and **Kepler-186f**, habitable zone comparisons, and the handover to TESS and JWST.
